About Parampara Farms

Where learning comes alive, beyond the classroom

Parampara Farms began as ancestral farmland in Karepura village, Doddaballapura Taluk, on the rural edge of Bengaluru. Today it is a working organic farm reimagined as a place for children to learn the way our grandparents did — by doing, growing and getting their hands into the soil.

Every visit is built around real farm work and real heritage: pulling vegetables from the earth, shaping clay on a potter's wheel, feeding the animals, and playing the games that filled village courtyards for generations. Nothing here is a simulation — it's a working farm that happens to teach.

Learning Beyond the Classroom

More than just a farm visit

Five commitments shape every programme we run — the same five a teacher or parent can expect from the first phone call to the last goodbye circle.

Experiential Learning

Hands-on activities mapped to NEP 2020's push for real-world, competency-based learning — not another worksheet.

Farm to Life Connection

Children harvest what they eat and meet the animals that feed us, building a lasting respect for where food actually comes from.

Safe & Secure Environment

Trained farm guides, first-aid stations, defined group ratios and hygiene protocols at every zone of the property.

Heritage & Culture

Pottery, traditional games and storytelling keep Karnataka's rural traditions alive for a generation growing up online.

Fun-Filled Experiences

Water slides, rain dances and bullock cart rides mean the learning never feels like a lesson — it feels like a great day out.

200+ Capacity

Purpose-built zones let us host full-day school batches without a single activity feeling crowded or rushed.
