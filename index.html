How a project runs

A simple sequence, with something you can review at the end of every step.

  1. Discover

    We talk through the goal, the users and the limits of budget and time.

  2. Specify

    You get a short specification, a rough architecture and a plan you can approve.

  3. Prototype

    Clickable screens or a working front end, so you can react before the back end is built.

  4. Build

    Short cycles with regular demos, tested as we go.

  5. Launch and support

    Deployment, handover and help after go-live.
